Natural gas prices in the United States spiked sharply in 2022 following
Russia's invasion of Ukraine, which disrupted global energy markets. Because
natural gas is the marginal fuel for electricity generation in many regions,
wholesale and retail electricity prices rose in tandem. Prices began to
moderate in 2023 as gas supply recovered and demand eased. Regional differences
remain significant: the Northeast and California typically pay above the
national average for retail electricity, while the Pacific Northwest benefits
from abundant hydropower.
